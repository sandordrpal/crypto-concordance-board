What followed each state in the last 30 days

For every coin and every past hour, the board rebuilds the state using only data available at that hour, then looks at what the price did next. This is the test of whether the states carry information.

    S · SentimentFear & Greed Index is 25 or lower (extreme fear).It is 75 or higher (extreme greed).

    The two scores

    • Net is up votes minus down votes, as a share of the families with data. It runs from −100 to +100.
    • Discord is the share of non-flat votes on the losing side, doubled. 0 means nobody disagrees. 100 means an even split.
    • Exchange gap is the distance between the highest and lowest price across Binance, OKX and Bybit, in basis points (1 bp = 0.01%).

    Limits

    • The voting rules are plain heuristics. They have not been shown to predict prices.
    • Stretch, positioning and sentiment are read as contrarian, which is a convention and not a proven effect.
    • The families are not independent, so agreement can look stronger than it is.
    • On-chain data, ETF flows and macro releases are not included because they have no free feed a browser can call.
    Market data: public endpoints of Binance, OKX and Bybit. Sentiment: Crypto Fear & Greed Index by alternative.me. Information only. Nothing here is investment advice, and no state on this board is a recommendation to buy or sell.
